▶ Phase 1: Revenue-Critical Fixes

Procedural Call Engine REVENUE
The IAP that doesn't work yet. InfiniteSignal.ts has a TODO where procedural generation should be. Build a ProceduralCallGenerator that creates calls from templates + variation rules per band. Each generated call gets a unique callerId, procedural lines from fragment libraries, and randomized sanityDelta/staticReward within band-appropriate ranges.

15 tapes, zero playback. TapePlayback.ts exists but tapes are metadata-only. Add synthesized audio per tape (ambient drones, voice fragments, static textures) using the existing VoiceProcessor. Each tape should sound distinct by band and rarity. This completes the core collect → listen loop.

▶ Phase 2: Content Depth

~3 weeks · 4 tasks
New Call Types ENGAGEMENT
Only 5 types. Room for 5+ more:
  • CONVERSATION — two-way dialogue, player taps responses from options
  • PUZZLE — multi-step decode with escalating complexity
  • TIMING — rhythm-based, respond at exact moments
  • MULTI_CALLER — two voices on one call, choose who to engage
  • RECORDING — play back found footage, then respond
Each needs a handler in engine/calls/renderers/ + component in components/calls/.
GameplayEngine
Meta-Narrative Arc RETENTION
The whistleblower (#14) reveals a 47-station network. The Origin (#15) from 1923 knows your name. These are story seeds. Build a hidden narrative layer: calls unlock in a specific order, choices in RIGHT_ANSWER calls affect which future calls appear, and collecting all 15 tapes triggers a meta-ending. The "relay point" concept from call #14 could mean the player is part of something larger.
StoryNarrative
Player Choice Persistence REPLAYABILITY
Choices don't matter beyond the call. RIGHT_ANSWER outcomes affect sanity/static/tapes but not future calls. Add a ChoiceHistory store: track key decisions, have callers reference past choices ("You said you'd help. Did you mean it?"), and unlock branching call paths. Could enable multiple endings.
StoryEngine
New Bands & Call Content CONTENT
5 bands, 18 calls — content is finite. Add:
  • WEATHER band — environmental horror, storms, nature
  • PIRATE band — rogue broadcasters, underground signals
  • HISTORICAL band — broadcasts from different eras
  • Expand each existing band from 3-4 calls to 8-10
  • Seasonal/event calls tied to real calendar dates

▶ Phase 3: Engagement & Retention

~3 weeks · 4 tasks
Daily Mystery Call RETENTION
No reason to open the app daily. Add a daily call that's unique per calendar day (seeded RNG). Available for 24h only. Some daily calls are exclusive — can't be received any other way. Creates FOMO and a daily habit. Track "daily streak" as an achievement.
EngagementContent
Player Identity & DJ Persona IMMERSION
Origin call (#15) says your name — but you never enter it. Add player name entry on first launch. Use it in select calls (Origin, "You Called Us", meta-narrative). Let players choose a DJ call sign and station name. The radio body could display the station name. Deepens personal investment.
ImmersionFeature
Onboarding & Tutorial CONVERSION
Splash just says "tap to begin." No tutorial, no explanation. New players land on radio with no context. Add a 3-call tutorial sequence: first call explains tuning, second explains answering, third explains choices. Use LIVING band calls with gentle sanity impact. After tutorial, real game begins.
FeatureUX
Difficulty System DEPTH
Store has difficulty setting, unclear if it does anything. Implement 3 modes:
  • NIGHT OWL (easy) — slower sanity drain, more static tolerance
  • INSOMNIAC (normal) — current balance
  • NO REST (hard) — faster calls, harsher sanity, permadeath
Difficulty affects CallScheduler timing + SanityEffectEngine thresholds.

▶ Phase 5: Polish & Scale

~2 weeks · 3 tasks
Environmental Audio System IMMERSION
No weather, no room tone, no environmental variation. Add a layered ambient system: base room tone + weather (random per shift) + sanity-driven distortion. Low sanity = room tone shifts, whispers bleed in, CRT warps harder. Weather affects signal quality (rain = more static). Creates organic variety per session.
AudioAtmosphere
New Game+ & Endless Mode REPLAYABILITY
Once 15 tapes collected, game is done. No replay hook. Add:
  • New Game+ — restart with all bands unlocked, harder calls, new tape variants
  • Endless Night — no tape goal, just survive. Calls get progressively stranger
  • Tape Mastery — re-listen to tapes to unlock hidden content layers
