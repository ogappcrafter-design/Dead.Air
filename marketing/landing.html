The Signal

  • 📻
    Radio Tuning

    Dial through 5 unlockable frequency bands. Living. Liminal. Lost. Classified. And the one we don't name. Static responds to every choice.

  • 📞
    Real-Time Calls

    18 hand-crafted transmissions across 5 call types — Just Listen, Dead Air, Right Answer, Signal Decode, Stay Calm. Nothing repeats the same way twice.

  • 📼
    Tape Collection

    A 15-tape archive to collect across a full playthrough. Every tape is a memory worth keeping — and some are worth forgetting.

  • 🌀
    Sanity Effects

    A real-time sanity system. The dial drifts. The signal degrades. No two sessions tune in to the same radio. Your choices carry weight.

  • ♾️
    Infinite Signal

    Optional IAP ($3.99). Endless AI-generated calls, never the same twice. Keeps the frequency open after the base game ends.
